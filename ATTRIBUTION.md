## ATCF best-track data

The best-track files used by the track tutorials are ATCF/HURDAT2 data from
NOAA/NHC. NOAA/NHC data are U.S. Government works and are redistributed here
with attribution. NOAA does not endorse this project.

## Station data

`Adding Map Features with Cartopy/water_level_stations.csv` contains example
water-level station coordinates used for the Cartopy tutorial. The station IDs
correspond to NOAA/NOS water-level stations.

## RiverGages data

`Calculating River Flow/cera_river_flow.ipynb` fetches historical stage data
from the USACE RiverGages portal at runtime. No RiverGages data files are
redistributed in this repository.

## Publication

The river-flow tutorial uses digitized data points from:

> Roberts, H. J., Westerink, H. J., & Westerink, L. G. (2013). Simulating
